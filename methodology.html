Methodology

How the record is kept

The Observatory is a daily, country-level record of how artificial intelligence is changing jobs, access and infrastructure in Africa. It is a product of Astrolabe Data.

Three dimensions

  • Jobs. How AI is changing employment, digital work and economic opportunity.
  • Access. Whether individuals and businesses can afford and access AI technologies.
  • Infrastructure. Whether Africa has the computing, connectivity, electricity and data-centre capacity to participate.

Sourcing

Every entry in the tracker links to its original source. Entries are drawn from established media, research organisations, company announcements and government communications, and are categorised by dimension at the point of entry. Where an item speaks to more than one dimension it is filed under the dominant one.

Dates

Each item carries the date of the underlying event or publication. Items are ordered newest first; entries more than one calendar year old are archived.

Independence

The Observatory is owned by Astrolabe Africa, an independent African media and research organisation. It accepts no payment in exchange for coverage or placement. Sponsor and partnership revenue is disclosed where it applies.

Full source data is downloadable at data/items.json. The detailed definitions and update log move to this page in the next phase of the redesign.
