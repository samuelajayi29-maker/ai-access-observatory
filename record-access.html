How this record is kept

The standard workload

Input plus a quarter of output, a defined unit rather than an average bill. Prices are list rates from provider documentation or reporting. A rate card is not a cost: a model that finishes a task in fewer steps can cost less than a cheaper model.

Capability

Scores come from the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. The current snapshot uses November 2026 v5.0.1; launch-era scores are not comparable with current ones. Each row carries its basis.

Known gaps

The official location lists cover 6 provider-product lists; they describe geographic listing, not signup, local payment, feature-tier access or uptime. A GNI-based workload comparison has values for 52 of 54 countries. The separate minimum-wage comparison covers 8 countries in its declared sample. African providers and locally hosted inference are not yet priced. Device records measure reported changes, not an African price index.
