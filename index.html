AI ACCESS OBSERVATORY

Africa’s ability to participate in the AI economy — measured, not reported.

Africa is entering a period of rapid technological transformation, driven by artificial intelligence, automation and digital infrastructure. Most of what’s written about it is fragmented, produced for a global audience, and difficult to verify from an African vantage point.

This record exists to close that gap. It examines Africa’s ability to participate in the AI economy across three dimensions — Jobs, Access and Infrastructure.

Built for policymakers, researchers, journalists and development institutions who need a number they can verify, not a headline they have to trust.

derived A modelled occupational estimate of today, not an observed count of displaced workers. This pillar still has no register.

What we track

Every figure above carries its evidence status:

  • measured observed, in service, actual
  • announced committed or named, not yet real
  • forecast a third party's projection
  • proposal proposed policy, not enacted
  • derived computed by us from other rows
  • modelled a third party's modelled estimate of a state: computed from published inputs
