Scope and coverage

  • What is in scope. Colocation and hyperscale facilities of 1 MW and above, plus nationally announced cloud and AI compute programmes, each traceable to a named source.
  • What is out of scope. Enterprise self-build, sites below 1 MW, and unannounced facilities. Published estimates run from 125 to 230 facilities depending on definition; this ledger carries 19 identifiable sites and programmes, so it is a partial record by design.
  • Status is never mixed. Operational, building, approved, announced and exploring are kept apart, because announced capacity is not capacity. 16 rows have no published MW and are left blank rather than estimated.
  • Capital is separated from capacity. Portfolio raises are their own rows, government targets are flagged as targets, and the two currencies of this module, MW and USD, are never summed.
  • Capacity is not utilisation. African markets take six to eight years to reach 85% occupancy, against two to three in Europe, so installed capacity can sit empty.
