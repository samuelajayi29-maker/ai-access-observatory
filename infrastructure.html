        Supporting charts and methods

        How this record is kept

        What counts as a project

        Named facilities and announced projects are recorded with their stated scope. Colocation resold from another operator’s floor is excluded where it would count the same megawatt twice. Portfolio capital raises are not site capacity.

        What counts as serving load

        A facility is treated as operational when the record has evidence it serves customer load. Announcements, permits and ribbon-cuttings do not count as serving capacity.

        Known gaps

        17 of 22 project rows carry no published megawatt figure. Shared or pan-African capacity is not split between countries. Published continental estimates put Africa at 360 MW active and 238 MW under construction, so this register covers only a fraction of the market.
