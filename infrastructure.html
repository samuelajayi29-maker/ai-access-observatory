        How this record is kept

        What counts as a project

        Named facilities and announced projects are recorded with their stated scope. Colocation resold from another operator’s floor is excluded where it would count the same megawatt twice. Portfolio capital raises are not site capacity.

        What counts as stated capacity at opened facilities

        A facility is treated as operational when the record has evidence it serves customer load. Announcements, permits and ribbon-cuttings do not count as serving capacity.

        Known gaps

        16 of 22 project rows carry no published megawatt figure. Shared or pan-African capacity is not split between countries. Continental estimates (360 MW active and 238 MW under construction) have a different scope and reporting snapshot. They cannot be compared with this register’s total across delivery statuses.

        Context indicators

        Conditions around the AI economy

        General economic and infrastructure conditions, with their own units and observation years. These figures do not measure the effect of AI. Internet use describes connectivity, not the number of people who can or will use AI. Electricity prices cover specified customers and tariffs; 53 countries have no price in this collection.

        Access to electricity

        54/54 countries with a value; 0 with no information.

        Access to electricity is the percentage of population with access to electricity. Electrification data are collected from industry, national surveys and international sources.

        Compare countries and years

        Electricity generating capacity

        54/54 countries with a value; 0 with no information.

        Total electricity generating capacity reported by EIA (electricity product 2, capacity activity 7). Million kilowatts converted to MW by multiplying by 1,000. Capacity is potential power, not annual generation or electricity consumed. No inference of dependable capacity or power available to data centres.

        Eskom Homelight 20A electricity rate

        1/54 countries with a value; 53 with no information.

        Published flat active-energy charge for Eskom direct Homelight 20A customers in South Africa, VAT included. Cents per kWh divided by 100 to obtain ZAR/kWh. Not a national average, municipal resale, industrial tariff or modelled household bill. Applicable tariff years start on 1 April.
