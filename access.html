Method

  • Two kinds of number. Prices come from providers or named outlets; capability from the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Every row links to its source.
  • The index was restated mid-September. Launch-era scores sit on an older scale, so the basis is recorded per row and only the current one is plotted.
  • Rate card is not cost. Astra is reported to match Fable 5.1 at roughly 40% of the cost per completed task, because it finishes in fewer steps. Peak windows, batch rates and resold access all sit below list.

Method

  • Device prices are one-off costs and stay separate from token prices. A cheaper model does not help someone who cannot afford the handset.
  • Forecasts are labelled. Analyst projections and a proposed Kenyan excise duty are marked as such, never stated as outcomes.
  • Component costs are the shared cause. Memory prices pass through to device prices, which is how AI demand shows up in African shop prices.
