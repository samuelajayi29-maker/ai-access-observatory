About this page

Generated, not written

Every figure here is pulled from the indicator registry, which is built from the registers. The page exists because the record measures something about Nigeria; it does not fill the gaps to look complete, and a pillar with no entry reports data unavailable rather than zero.

Where the numbers come from

Each indicator states its own source, date, evidence status and the file it can be recomputed from. Country profiles are the same data as the series pages, arranged by country instead of by theme.
